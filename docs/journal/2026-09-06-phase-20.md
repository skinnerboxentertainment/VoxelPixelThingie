**What was built.** `MemoryIndex` (entries by event, an inverted index of
tokens from causes, annotation values, and passport text; structured
filters by scan; a stable file with the manifest seq), `buildIndex`,
`loadOrBuildIndex` with staleness by seq, `tokenize` and `tokensOf`;
the `search` workload in `src/actor.ts` whose audit is a scan without
the index; the MCP `search` tool, built from the store once and extended
with the session's events; `scripts/scene-search.ts`; `tests/memory.test.ts`
and a case in `tests/mcp-scene.test.ts`.

**Two things the oracles caught.** A ledger can be one step ahead of the
manifest (ledger first, §10.6), so an index built from the ledgers can
hold an event the manifest's seq does not cover; the index now sits at
the later of the two and ignores a (bit, seq) it has seen. And a search
job found its own request, because the request's params contain the
words being searched for; the workload now leaves the job's own records
out of the history it searches.

**Sizes.** The reference scene's index is 10.1 MB, most of it the token
postings for 54,392 events; it is written beside the manifest, outside
the seal and the pack, and rebuilt in 1.3 s when absent. Vectors remain
a declared slot with nothing behind it.

**Not done.** No ranking: hits are ordered by bit then seq, and text
matching is every-word. No index over emissions' colors or positions;
those are state, not memory, and the container answers them directly.
