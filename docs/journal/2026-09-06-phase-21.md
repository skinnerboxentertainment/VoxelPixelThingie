**What was built.** `Reading`, the quantity table (temperature CEL,
illuminance LUX, humidity P1, pressure PAL, touch C62), the WLED
`info.sensor` mapping with unit strings to UN/CEFACT codes,
`recordReadings`; `validateSenseAnnotation` beside the job and policy
checks in the sink; the sense branch in `toEpcisEvent` with a
`deviceUri` helper; the last-reading rule in `compactLedger`; the
twin's drifting `sensor` array; `readWledSensors` in the bridge module
and the polling loop in the driver; `tests/senses.test.ts`.

**Decisions (ADR 0015).** Readings are annotations, not a new event
type; units are UN/CEFACT codes and types are CBV so the export uses
the standard sensor fields and no extension; the device is the actor;
compaction keeps the last reading per quantity. The WLED sensor API is a
draft in its own documentation; the letter mapping is Trusted until a
usermod build reports through it on the physical bit.
