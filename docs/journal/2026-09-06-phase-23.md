**What the oracles corrected.** Two expectations were mine, not the
model's: the validator counts a mesh's triangles once however many nodes
share it, and a data-only emission changes no color or light, so the
seam bit shares the reference material. One was the scene's: the 4³
reference carves its corner from (1,1,1), so (3,3,3) is destroyed and
the moved-node test had to pick a present bit.

**What was built.** Export (a unit cube with flat normals, one mesh per
material, `emissiveFactor` from the mean face light, the extension only
above one, `extras.vpb` on nodes and scene, the pack gzipped through
`CompressionStream`), GLB packing and parsing, import by pack or by
nodes with `moved` detection, the two scripts, the Blender script, the
validator as a dev dependency with a local type declaration,
`tests/gltf.test.ts`.

**Not done.** No per-face materials: a node is one material, the mean of
its faces' light. No web-viewer automation: Blender and the validator
are the oracles; the Three.js demo does not load glTF. A tool that drops
unknown extras loses the pack; the importer then rebuilds from the
nodes, which the second oracle covers.
