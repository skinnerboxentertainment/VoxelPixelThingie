**What was built.** `src/jobs.ts` (the three records plus the optional
reward, validation, `jobsOf`), `src/storage.ts` (the `Storage` contract,
CIDv1 raw SHA-256 ids, memory and verifying backends) and
`src/storage-node.ts` (a folder backend), `src/actor.ts` (the `Actor` and
`ActorPool` contracts, three workloads with checks that can fail, the
in-process pool with one job at a time per bit), `scripts/job-drive.ts`,
the sink validator, and the EPCIS mapping.

**Decisions (ADR 0010).** No new event type: job records are annotations
under reserved keys, so containers and exporters are untouched and only
the sink learned to validate. The order request, result, audit, reward is
fixed, and a reward follows only a passed audit. The business step in the
EPCIS export is the record's step, not the wrangler's cause, so a
supply-chain reader sees `job-audit` rather than "audit links".

**Found on the way.**

- The first EPCIS test counted half the job observations: the wrangler's
  cause ("job links", "audit links") had become the business step and
  only half matched `job-`. The step is now the record's own, whatever
  the cause said.
- The first cut inlined byte results under 4 KiB as a JSON array. The
  204-number LED frame inside an EPCIS extension made OpenEPCIS fail the
  whole chunk with an empty "Translation failed". Bytes now always go to
  storage by content id; only JSON values ride inline. Found by capturing
  the twelve job records one at a time.

**OpenEPCIS capture, Verified this session.** A copy of the small scene
with three jobs on its first bit (1,386 EPCIS events, 12 of them job
records) captured into OpenEPCIS CE in 14 chunks, 44.9 s, eight in
flight; the query returned exactly 1,386. Paging through them, the job
observations read back as 3 each of `job-request`, `job-result`,
`job-audit`, `job-reward`, with the audit's `vpb:job` sensor report
carrying the check in words. OpenEPCIS CE does not filter on a custom
business step (`EQ_bizStep` tries to translate it as an EPC and fails
with a 500), so the count was made client-side over the pages.
